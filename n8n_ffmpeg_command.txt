### EXPLANATION OF CHANGES:

1. **Background Layer**: [0:v] (in_1) scaled to 1920x1080, used throughout entire video
2. **Foreground Images**: [1:v], [2:v], [3:v] are scaled to max 1400px width/787px height (fits within letterbox area)
3. **Zoom Effect**: zoompan filter with gentle 5% zoom over 5 seconds (z='min(1.0+0.05*(on/125),1.05)')
4. **Rounded Corners**: geq filter creates circular corners (40px radius) - Option 1 only
5. **Letterbox Bars**: drawbox adds 80px black bars at top (y=0) and bottom (y=1000)
6. **Overlay Timing**:
   - fg1 appears from 0-5 seconds
   - fg2 appears from 5-10 seconds
   - fg3 appears from 10-15 seconds
7. **Centered**: overlay=(W-w)/2:(H-h)/2 centers each foreground image
8. **Aspect Ratio**: scale filter preserves original aspect ratio

### CUSTOMIZATION PARAMETERS:

- **Letterbox height**: Change h=80 to adjust bar size (current: 80px top + 80px bottom = 160px total)
- **Zoom amount**: Change 0.05 to 0.1 for stronger zoom (min(1.0+0.1*(...),1.1) = 10% zoom)
- **Zoom speed**: Change d=125 (frames) to control zoom duration at 25fps
- **Rounded corner radius**: Change 40 in geq formula for larger/smaller corners
- **Foreground size**: Change 1400x787 to scale foreground images larger/smaller
- **Duration**: Change -t 5 for each input, and adjust enable times accordingly
